TASKLY CODES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the current version 1.0.0 of Taskly Codes, a Flutter app for searching equipment error codes and using the optional AI Assistant. It covers the app itself, not other Taskly products or this website.

2. Accounts and information we collect

Taskly Codes does not create or require an account, login, password, profile, or user registration. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment details.

When you use the app, it can store the following app data locally on your device: favorites, a local history of viewed codes, notes attached to codes, language and dark-mode preferences, and the local count used for the AI quota. The code history is limited to the latest 50 entries. This data is not uploaded by the app.

3. AI Assistant and network requests

The error-code catalogue works locally. If you choose to use AI Assistant and the app is built with a working Gemini API key, the app sends the category, selected symptom, your description, and selected language to Google Gemini through the google_generative_ai SDK. The app asks Gemini for a structured safety classification; it does not send your saved favorites, history, notes, or account information as part of this request.

AI output is an unverified safety-oriented classification, not a repair instruction or professional diagnosis. If AI is not configured or the request cannot be completed, no AI response is stored by the app.

Google may process a request according to its own terms and privacy practices. Review Google's Privacy Policy before entering information into AI Assistant.

4. Analytics, crash reporting, and SDKs

Version 1.0.0 contains no configured analytics service, advertising SDK, crash-reporting service, or telemetry endpoint. Analytics is disabled by default, no analytics sink is configured, and the app does not queue usage events for later upload.

The app uses Flutter, shared_preferences for local storage, url_launcher for opening the Taskly Report PRO App Store page, and the Google Generative AI SDK for the optional AI request.

5. External links

The app can open the Taskly Report PRO App Store listing in your device's external browser or App Store app. External services have their own privacy policies. Taskly Codes does not receive data from the App Store through this link.

6. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it using the app's available clear-data controls, your device's app settings, or by uninstalling the app. We do not maintain a Taskly Codes account database or a server-side copy of this local data.

7. GDPR rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have the right to request access to, rectification of, erasure of, or restriction of processing of personal data concerning you; to object to processing; and, where applicable, to receive data in a portable format. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority.

Because the current app keeps its normal data locally and does not create accounts, we generally cannot access or identify that data remotely. For questions about a support email or an AI request, contact us and provide enough detail for us to locate the correspondence without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or rights requests, email iurigutu@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.
